Player by Index of Luck ?

Index of Luck Formula:
(Lucky Wins + 1) / (Unlucky Losses + 1*)
Lucky Wins: Wins on tiebreakers.
Unlucky Losses: Potential wins missed due to tiebreakers.
Higher values suggest that player is more "lucky".
If index equals to 1 number of player's lucky wins and unlucky losses are the same.
*Counts are increased by 1 to handle zero values and avoid division by zero.
